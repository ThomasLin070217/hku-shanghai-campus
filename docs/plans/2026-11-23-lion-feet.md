## What must change

1. The authored gold paws are the feet. `Paw` + `Paw_toe` + paw lettering/crest
   bind to `footL/footR`, not to the forearms.
2. The lower-body dark rounds (`Trouser_leg`) are the pant leg, not a foot:
   bind them to the thigh. They stay exactly in place in the seated statue
   because the seated clip is the bind pose.
3. Delete the added human foot and toes (`Lion_rig_foot_*`, `Lion_rig_toe_*`).
4. Re-seat the leg skeleton behind the paws (no ±.36 narrow stance, no
   straight-down humanoid rest), and replace the thin cylinders with stocky
   trouser-colored leg segments that connect hip → knee → ankle → paw.
5. Re-author the leg rotations for every clip around a new standing baseline,
   keeping the paws level and their soles on the ground (no float, no sink).

## Rig v2 — concrete values (body-local, `Lion_body_uniform_scale` space)

Rest chain (bind pose = the seated statue; **identity bone rotations must leave
the statue pixel-identical**):

| Bone | Left | Right | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| thigh | [−.66, .84, .10] | [.66, .84, .10] | hip joint inside the gown |
| shin | [−.72, .54, .48] | [.72, .54, .48] | knee inside `Trouser_leg` |
| foot | [−.76, .52, .86] | [.76, .52, .86] | ankle inside the back half of `Paw` |

- Chain lengths: thigh .488, shin .382 (total .870).
- `STAND` hips stay `[0, .55, −.02]` (hip y 1.39). With the chain aligned
  straight down, the ankle lands at y .522 and z .08 (hip z .10 − .02), i.e.
  the paw sole stays at y 0 and the toes sit ~.85 m in front of the body
  centre. Solved standing leg rotations: **thigh +.902, shin +.616,
  foot −1.518 rad** (foot cancels the chain, so the paw stays level).
- Foot compensation in every pose: `foot = −(thigh + shin)` (plus a small
  toe-drop when the foot is airborne). Never hand-author the foot value without
  the cancellation.
- `seated` clip: all leg rotations **0** (bind pose), hips offset 0. The
  awaken clip interpolates 0 → STAND for both legs (small left/right stagger
  allowed, ~.12 s).
- `Trouser_leg` on the thigh; `Seated_trousers` stays on the hips.
- Added geometry per side: a stocky thigh cylinder (hip→knee, radius ~.30→.27),
  a shin cylinder (knee→ankle, radius ~.27→.23), both `Lion dark trousers`,
  and a small ankle cuff at the foot bone. All three are hidden inside the
  gown/`Trouser_leg`/`Paw` in the seated pose. No foot or toe meshes are added.
- `boneForTrace`: `Paw*` → `footL/R` by `centerX`; `Trouser_leg` → `thighL/R`.
  `Arm`, `Gown_cuff`, `Gown_sleeve` stay on the arm bones. Remove `Paw*` from
  `FOREARM_PARTS`; add `FOOT_PARTS`; remove `Trouser_leg` from `HIPS_PARTS`.

Gait: every pose keeps the STAND baseline and adds deltas. The implementer
tunes amplitudes against two constraints: the paw sole never goes below
−0.01 m, and the stance paw is within 0.02 m of the ground at least once per
cycle. Walk/run cycle metres (`WALK_CYCLE_METRES`, `RUN_CYCLE_METRES`) are
re-measured from the paw's forward travel and updated.

### Task 3 — re-author the clips

Files: `src/lionBoss/lionRig.ts`.

- Add `STAND_THIGH = .902`, `STAND_SHIN = .616` and a `legRotations(thigh,
  shin)` helper returning `[thigh, shin, −(thigh + shin)]`; `STAND` carries the
  baseline for both legs.
- `SEATED` legs = 0; `awaken` uses a two-stage recipe verified numerically:
  (1) 0 → .35 s lift the hips to `[0,.55,−.02]` with the legs still at
  identity, so both paws leave the ground by .55 (no sink);
  (2) .35 → ~1.9 s extend thigh/shin to the STAND baseline (feet stay level
  via the cancellation, the sole descends monotonically to 0), with a ~.12 s
  stagger so the second foot plants after the first. A naive linear
  interpolation of hips and legs together sinks the feet ~.12 m at t=.5 —
  do not use one.
- `idle` keeps STAND + breath; `walk`/`run` swing around the baseline with
  knee lift and level feet; `chargeWarn`/`charge`/`swipeWarn`/`stunned`/
  `shutdown` add their deltas to the baseline.
- Update `WALK_CYCLE_METRES` / `RUN_CYCLE_METRES` from measured paw travel.
- Remove the old "rest legs hang straight; the seated clip folds them" comment
  and describe the new rest/bind semantics.
